# AI Cost Anomaly Explainer

## Overview

This layer uses AI to translate cost anomalies into clear engineering and business explanations.

The goal is not just to detect that cost changed, but to explain why it changed and what action should be taken.

---

## Example Inputs

- Service name
- Account ID
- Cost change %
- Usage change %
- Resource owner
- Historical trend
- Recent deployment context

---

## Example Output

- Plain-English summary of what changed
- Likely cost driver
- Suggested engineering action
- Business impact
- Jira-ready ticket description

---

## Outcome

This layer helps reduce investigation time and turns cost anomalies into actionable recommendations.
